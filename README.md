One line that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting — an entry
for [The Meeting That Should Have Been One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-one-line-a4af6d1c?ref=qrg7fvky)
on [make.fun](https://make.fun/?ref=qrg7fvky).

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Revenue is up, the hire starts Monday, and the only thing left to discuss is why we booked this room.

101 characters, one sentence. It shares the two pieces of news a status
meeting exists to share, then takes away the only reason left to gather.
